## Platform

## Stack

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and collaborators evaluating Đào Hoa Nữ for full-stack development, automation, AI integration, or digital product work.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that turns the CV into a clear, memorable proof of capability and makes it easy to contact Đào Hoa Nữ or download the full CV.

## Positioning

Đào Hoa Nữ combines product-minded full-stack development with automation, AI integrations, machine-learning research, and visual communication.

## Operating Context

Visitors usually scan quickly on desktop or mobile and need to understand role, experience, technical range, evidence, and contact details without reading a traditional CV linearly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Content is based on the supplied one-page CV. Do not invent clients, performance metrics, project outcomes, or links beyond the supplied contact and research link. Keep the page responsive and accessible.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name Đào Hoa Nữ, Vietnamese-first copy, and the supplied profile photo/contact details where helpful. Voice should be confident, direct, and human.

## Evidence on Hand

`CV-DaoHoaNu-0967223771.pdf` contains the source material: MiTelAI JSC experience from 06/2024 to present; an Information Technology degree from Đại học An Giang (2020–2024, GPA 3.51/4); research on COVID-19 prognosis using deep learning; Google Project Management and VSTEP B1 certificates; and a Good Graduation distinction.

## Product Principles

- Make capability legible in seconds.
- Show range without flattening the person into a tool list.
- Treat research and automation as proof of curiosity and depth.
- Make contact immediate and low-friction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic landmarks, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, alt text, and reduced-motion support.
